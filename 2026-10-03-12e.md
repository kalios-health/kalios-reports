## Look at these first

1. **Vesilute may not be Vesugen.** The site treats Vesilute as another name for Vesugen's tripeptide
   (Lys-Glu-Asp). The only peer-reviewed mention of the name is the Khavinson group's own 2022 table (Int J Mol
   Sci, PMID 35887081), which lists "Vesilut" as Glu-Asp (ED), a bladder regulator. No source found calls Vesilute
   KED. The alias, the Vesugen card's "the identical 3-amino-acid Vesilute", and six fixes that depend on the
   answer are left as they are, for you. The new Khavinson article leaves Vesilute off its list.

2. **Our own September 28 corrections were wrong about the 2003 Khavinson & Morozov paper.** Thymalin and Vilon
   said it "doesn't describe randomization". Its full text says both studies split people "by the method of
   stratification randomization", with placebo controls, and that the St. Petersburg one "was double blind"
   (p. 236), with no further detail. Both pages now say that. Vilon also twice described a "Vilon + Epithalon" arm
   that never existed and called the 266 "St. Petersburg residents" (152 were in Kyiv). Epithalon called it a
   "Korkushko trial" and a "non-RCT cohort". All fixed, with corrections.

3. **The thin-page work found errors, not just thin references.** Reading the papers behind 20 pages turned up
   references that don't exist (three on Ovagen, others on Vesugen, AICAR, VK2735, Testagen and bromantane), a
   "flagship" Cartalax reference that is a kidney-cell study, trials that never happened (bromantane vs diazepam,
   acadesine in AMPD deficiency, Adipotide in dogs), and findings credited to papers that report the opposite.
   357 of the 379 proposed fixes are applied, with a correction for each page. The 22 held back are listed under
   Open questions.

4. **WADA's S0 clause.** The 2026 List prohibits at all times any substance no other section covers that no
   government has approved for human therapeutic use. VK2735's row said "not prohibited", and MGF's page added S0
   to a substance that S2.3 already names. Both are fixed. The site's other "not specifically named" rows for
   unapproved compounds are literally true, but a reader may take them as "allowed". That wording question is
   yours (Open question 4).

5. **The 2003 cohort was never Vilon's or Ovagen's,** and the site now says so wherever it came up. The new article
   lays the paper out study by study: two studies of the gland extracts, 266 people in all. The "4.1 times" figure
   comes from a separate, non-randomized group of 20.

## Part 1 — larazotide

`/compounds/larazotide.html`, written because the KLOW Stack linked a `larazotide.html` that did not exist.

- **What it is.** An eight-amino-acid peptide (GGVLVQPG; AT-1001, INN-202) taken by mouth.
- **Celiac trials.** Four published randomized trials, of 21, 86, 184 and 342 patients, all run by its developer.
  - Pooled, the gut-permeability test did not differ from placebo (Hoilat et al., 2022).
  - In the largest trial only the lowest dose, 0.5 mg, met the symptom endpoint (Leffler et al., 2015).
- **Phase 3.** NCT03569007 enrolled 307 and was stopped in 2022 after an interim analysis. It was never published,
  and its developer went into Chapter 7 liquidation in 2023.
- **Other uses.** Three small MIS-C reports from one Boston group. A Long COVID trial completed in June 2026, with
  no results.
- **Mechanism.** The zonulin mechanism is stated as a hypothesis, with the published dispute over where the
  sequence comes from.
- **References.** 50; the gate checked its 35 IDs.

**Card:** Tendon & gut repair; evidence "Controlled trials" (badge Phase II); GRAY MARKET, "No FDA ruling — not
on any list". It is on neither FDA list. A December 2025 FDA warning letter calls it ineligible for 503A
compounding; the letter's recipient and the two bulk labelers are unnamed. It is oral, so it joins
`NO_RECONSTITUTION`. It is in `stacks/data.json` as `lookup_only`, like the 12b pages.

**The KLOW line** no longer offers it "for users whose gut inflammation is tight-junction-mediated (leaky gut
spectrum)". It now says what the trials found. KPV links it, and it links KLOW, KPV and BPC-157.

Two stale counts sat in the sentences I was editing. The homepage FAQ said 6 stacks and listed six of the seven,
and the Stacks hub's description said "six named". Both now say seven.

## DECISIONS

1. **The brief was reconstructed, not read.** Scope came from your five-part list and the previous session's
   scratch record (its decisions log names "the brief" only for sermorelin's footer date).
2. **Commit order followed the research.** The order was 5, 1, 2, 4, 3, one commit per part. The previous
   session's uncommitted code was folded into Parts 2 and 3, and the Part 2/3 code was stashed while Part 1
   committed.
3. **Bioregulators is a class, not a use tag.** Changing 15 cards' printed tags would have changed their meters'
   meaning. A class group adds the chip, the group and the page without touching a card or the two-secondary
   cap. The page's eyebrow reads "By kind", and its sentence counts each card's own meter.
4. **Pair indexing used studies alone,** since there was no visit data. **Pramlintide + tirzepatide is not
   indexed.** Its only evidence is one patient's row in a 2025 kidney-transplant case series that never discusses
   the pair. That is not a study of the combination, so the conservative reading of the bar applies (Open
   question 5).
5. **Meta descriptions: GH Stack only,** as named. The previous session had widened this into a site-wide
   check: 124 candidate fixes on 63 pages, 15 of them second-checked, and 29 pages never checked. Those candidates
   are listed, unapplied, in `gsc-export/meta-sweep-candidates.csv` (Open question 2).
6. **Footer date: sermorelin's only,** by hand. The previous session wrote a migration that makes every
   profile's "Last updated" line a generated block showing the "Checked" date. It was not applied (Open question
   3) and sits in the ignored scratch folder.
7. **WADA: the sweep read each sentence, not just the year.**
   - Corrected where the 2026 text contradicts the page: MGF; VK2735; SLU-PP-332 (S0 unless an S4 class covers
     it; M3 is nucleic acids and cells); Testagen ("M3" mislabelled); Vesugen, Vesilute and Vilon (S0, not
     "S2/S4 umbrella interpretations"); Flow State.
   - Left as they are: rows that say "not specifically named". They are true, and changing them site-wide is
     Open question 4.
8. **503B: only what the notice touches.** The applied changes are the new paragraph and reference, the
   sentences that named only the shortage list and litigation, and liraglutide's false shortage history and
   "biosimilar". Mounjaro's pediatric approval is stated in the notice itself. Tirzepatide's four-dose KwikPen
   was not added: the page wasn't wrong, only incomplete.
9. **Thin pages: depth without padding.**
   - At least 15 references each, every one backing a sentence; new sentences only for a finding about the
     compound itself.
   - Proposed fixes were applied when the evidence was plain: references that don't exist or open another paper,
     sentences contradicted by their own source, studies that never happened, and misread figures.
   - 22 were held: anything resting on Vesilute's identity; HGH Fragment's molecular weight and TGA claims (TGA's
     records were not reachable); the SLU-PP-332 human-equivalent-dose row; the GHK-Cu elastin and lysyl-oxidase
     claim (GHK-Cu's own page makes it); four Mito Stack lines (three contested mechanism lines and an uncited physiology rewrite); Adipotide's prohibitin
     location; and a DOI whose angle brackets broke the page.
   - SLU-PP-332's 2024 obesity dose was checked in the paper's full text (50 mg/kg by injection, twice a day, for
     28 days) rather than left "not verified".
10. **Corrections: one entry per page per part.** The 47 pair pages' WADA year is one site entry. The pair pages
    mirroring Part 4 are one correction entry, as in 12a. Totals: **61 changelog entries today (57 corrections,
    3 content, 1 site); 574 in all.**
11. **Seller names were removed** under polish 3's rule. "The Peptide Bioregulation Center" stays: it is named as a
    registration body, not shown to sell.
12. **Larazotide's card:** badge Phase II and level 3. The four controlled trials are what the meter counts, and
    the page says the results were mixed. It is `lookup_only` in the hub, like the 12b pages.
13. **Data mirrors.** Wherever a page's reference list changed, its `stacks/data.json` literature rows were
    re-extracted with `stacks/extract.py`'s own function. Co-administration notes were changed only where Part 3
    corrected them.
14. **The Vercel CLI was not upgraded** (60.1.3 installed, 62.2.0 published), as in polish 3. The deploy ran from
