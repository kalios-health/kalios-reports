## Part 1 — dosing, protocol and monitoring (89 pages)

G's rule: a dose from a trial or a label is cited inline with its population; a community, compounded
or clinic figure is attributed and loses the instruction words; a figure with no source and no honest
attribution is cut; an efficacy threshold is cited or cut. No sentence in Kalios's voice tells the
reader to take, run, cycle, test, store or combine anything.

- **3,429 edits**: 1,248 cite, 794 attribute, 709 reword, 678 cut. The pages' own records count
  **5,317 items changed**: 1,108 cited, 786 attributed, 2,654 cut, 56 reworded.
- **66 optional sections** whose every line was cut are gone (What to Expect, Practical User Notes,
  Supportive Nutrition, Sample Weekly Schedule…). The 17 core sections all stay; where a core
  section lost everything it keeps one honest sentence ("No trial, label or guideline sets
  monitoring for X; the trials measured …"). Wolverine's heading is back to the template's.
- **Labels and trials read for the doses**, not remembered: the Japanese GHRP Kaken 100 label
  (pralmorelin's approved 100 µg IV test dose, its adverse-reaction frequencies, its
  pituitary-apoplexy warning, its storage), current DailyMed labels for the approved drugs,
  Pentapharm's Vialox fact sheets, the LL-37 leg-ulcer and foot-ulcer trials, eloralintide's Lancet
  Phase 2 and two Phase 1 papers, Drugs@FDA for Geref.
- **Where sources disagree the page says so** rather than picking one, as the playbook requires.

## DECISIONS

1. **The previous session's work was audited, not trusted.** Every prepared edit file was re-checked
   against the page as it stands, and the two whose text had moved were re-derived by hand. 23 meta
   candidates and 9 held fixes turned out to be already fixed by Parts 1 and 2; they are recorded as
   such rather than re-applied.
2. **Two citation-gate catches shipped with Part 1, not Part 2.** The tightened rule (an author named
   in a sentence must be one of the paper's) fails the old text, so pramlintide's Ratner citation and
   Semax's Dolotov citation moved into the Part 1 commit to keep every commit's gate green.
3. **Wave 2 was done as a single agent, by hand, page by page** (the brief's instruction). Two
   tools made it affordable: `_scratch/truth3/digest.py` puts one page's whole scope in one file, and
   `build.py` turns a compact spec into a validated edit file, checking every Part 2 unit's verdict
   against the edited page. 7 pages were finished this way; the interrupted agents' drafts for LL-37
   and GHRP-2 were read line by line, kept where sourced, and extended.
4. **Conservative on routes.** Cartalax's `studied` uses only the papers its page cites (rats, route
   not stated) rather than the intraperitoneal route from a paper the page does not cite.
5. **Attributions rest on the page's own naming or a document read this session.** The web-search
   allowance was exhausted in the previous session; where neither existed, the figure was cut (G's
   rule c). The pages where an attribution rests only on the page's own wording are listed in each
   page's edit file.
6. **A corrections entry per page per part**, except where the change is one site-wide fact: the pair
   pages, the stacking-card links and the WADA rows each have one entry instead of 89 or 58.
   **192 entries today** (191 corrections, 1 content); 766 in all.
7. **92 orphaned pair pages were retired, not left stale.** Under the locked Tier 1 rule a pair page
   exists only while a profile lists the pairing; those cards are gone, so the pages are too, each
   with three 301s to `/stack.html?a=…&b=…`. They were noindexed, so no indexed page was lost.
8. **Three code fixes shipped with the content.** `stacks/generate.py` lets the WADA cell print whole
   (G's clause was being cut mid-reason); `stacks/extract.py` no longer reads `compounds/index.html`
   as a compound, knows Tesa/Ipa is a stack, and skips Part 5's pair link when it reads a card.
9. **The full pair-data re-extract was discarded.** Running `extract.py` over everything rewrote 210
   hand-curated co-administration notes; the file was restored and only Vesilute's entry written.
10. **Follistatin-344 was stopped by the biology safeguard.** The edit being written was cut off
    mid-call. Nothing was written to the repo, and the page is listed below with the other 49.
11. **The Vercel CLI was not upgraded** (60.1.3 installed, 62.2.0 published), as in 12e and polish 3.
    The deploy ran from the documented git-less copy and needed two attempts: the first came back
    "Not authorized", the retry succeeded.
