## Look at these first

1. **The seam.** Every compound, stack and pair page ends its black shell with one mono label row — "THE READ" over "PAPER FOLLOWS YOUR PHONE · AA FLIPS IT", the 44 px Aa button at its right — and under it a torn edge, 18 px, the same path site-wide, bleeding as far as the paper does. Flip the Aa and the tear flips with the surface: cream with a hairline on paper, the shell's black with the tear drawn in gray at night. https://www.kalios.health/compounds/bpc-157.html
2. **The mark, three times and nowhere else.** The 404 ("Nothing here" / "The page moved, or never existed."), the `/compounds/` search with no match ("No page for that yet — if it's a real compound, Kalios hasn't written it up." with the way to be told when it exists), and small at the left of the receipt's "none published" scope line. https://www.kalios.health/this-page-does-not-exist
3. **The vial label's QR.** Printed or saved from the calculator after arriving from a compound page, the label carries a QR to that page with `?from=label`. It is drawn in the page by `assets/calc-qr.js` — byte mode, versions 1–6, level L, no service, no network. 33 modules across 0.56 in: a 0.43 mm module, 0.33 mm with the label printed 2 in wide. Opening a page with `?from=label` fires one Umami event, `label-scan`, with the compound as its only property, then drops the parameter.
4. **Three pages were wider than the phone, and the cause wasn't the table.** MariTide by 95 px, thymosin beta-4 by 35, exenatide by 15 — each from one word with nowhere to break (a peptide sequence, a long FDA address). All 204 pages now lay out at 390 px exactly.
5. **73 tap targets were under 44 px; 2 remain, by choice.**
6. **FOXO4-DRI's three flagged items are cut** (Part 0), after two sessions were stopped before reading the paper behind them.
7. **14 hero verdicts now agree with their card's meter** (addendum 1): BPC-157 reads "Limited Human Data" beside its three published human pilots, not "Preclinical". 23 disagreements stand, every one explained on the page.
8. **The GLP-1 group is "GLP-1 & Amylin Shots"** (addendum 2), its page at a new address with a 301 from the old one, and its sentence says what it holds: "Of 21 incretin and amylin drugs used for weight loss and the approved diabetes drugs they descend from, 4 are FDA-approved for that use and 15 more have controlled human trials."

### Part 1 — the seam (`8d5d1a41`)

- A new generated block, `sl:seam`, on 178 pages: 157 compound and stack pages, 21 pair pages. `streetlab.seam_html` draws it; `scripts/street_lab_migrate.py seam-blocks` put the empty block on existing pages; `stacks/generate.py` writes it on a pair page, which has no section nav, so the seam follows the receipt.
- The label row: two mono lines and the Aa button, **moved here from the References row**, with its behaviour and `aria-label` unchanged. The second half of line two ("· Aa flips it") shows only where the button does (`html.sl-js`): without JavaScript the button never appears, and the line must not promise it.
- The tear: one SVG, `viewBox="0 0 390 18"`, `preserveAspectRatio="none"`, the brief's path, drawn twice — filled with the reading surface's colour, then open and stroked 1 px along the tear alone, so no hairline crosses the paper at the bottom edge. The colours are tokens (`--seam-fill`, `--seam-line`), restated under the light media query and `[data-read="paper"]`, so the surface and the tear flip together.
- `html{overflow-x:clip}` keeps the 100vw tear from ever putting a horizontal scrollbar on a page where the system draws a classic vertical one.
- The seam is chrome, so no page's Checked date moved.

### Part 3 — the QR on the vial label (`46685994`)

- **`assets/calc-qr.js`**: a QR encoder, byte mode, versions 1–6, levels L and M. No library, no service, no network. It stops below version 7, where a symbol would also need its version block.
- **On the label**: the bottom-right corner, 0.56 in square, as inline SVG on the printed label and as modules on the canvas for the saved PNG. The mono lines are sized to stop short of it; the label's own white padding is the quiet zone.
- **No compound, no QR.** The calculator has no compound picker; the label's QR points at the compound page the visit came from (the referrer, as the `calc-run` event already used). A visit from anywhere else leaves the label as it was.
- **`?from=label`**: `assets/street-lab.js` gained a seventh job — one Umami event, `label-scan`, whose only property is the compound whose page it is, then `history.replaceState` drops the parameter. Never a dose, a date or anything the label said.
- **How it was checked while it was written:** every symbol was compared against an independent encoder (segno) and decoded from a rendered image (OpenCV), both in a throwaway virtual environment outside the repo. That comparison found a real bug: the format information was being placed with row and column swapped, which made every symbol undecodable. After the fix, the penalty scoring matches the reference exactly, all 157 compound URLs read back with the right payload and zero Reed-Solomon syndromes, and the saved label PNG decodes when resampled to a 2 in label at 600 and 300 dpi, with blur and noise added.
- **The gate keeps it:** `tests/calc-qr.test.mjs` (step 5 of `tests/run-calc-tests.sh`) redraws the recorded symbols in `tests/calc-qr-cases.json` module for module and reads each one back with a reader written from the standard, not from the encoder. 112 checks.
- `calculator-sw.js` caches the new file (`kalios-calc-v3`).

## DECISIONS

Conservative throughout. No questions asked.

**The seam**

- **D1. The scope is the brief's:** compound, stack and pair pages. The article pages that also carry a reading surface (About, the tracker, the guide, How we check, Alerts, the Khavinson article) keep their own transition, untouched.
- **D2. Only the black→paper cut gets a seam.** Where the paper ends and the shell resumes (under the References, above the author box) there is no tear: the brief asks for the opening, and a second tear would halve its meaning.
- **D3. The tear is drawn twice, filled and open.** A single closed path stroked all round would lay a hairline across the paper at the SVG's bottom edge. The open path carries the stroke, the closed one the fill, and both come from one string in `streetlab.py`, so they cannot drift.
- **D4. The tear bleeds 100vw, and `html` clips.** The paper bleeds to the viewport (its box-shadow), so a column-width tear would leave square paper corners on either side. 100vw is wider than the layout viewport where a classic scrollbar exists, so `overflow-x:clip` on `html` (not `hidden`: it makes no scroll container) keeps that from ever showing as a horizontal scrollbar.
- **D5. "· Aa flips it" is shown only where the button is.** Without JavaScript the Aa never appears, and the line would be a promise the page doesn't keep.

**The mark**

- **D6. The asset is referenced, not inlined.** `<img src="/art/mark.svg">` keeps one copy of the geometry — the one `scripts/mark.py` writes — and avoids two inline copies of the same filter ids on one page.
- **D7. The mark replaces Karl on the 404 and in the search's empty state.** Two marks in one empty state is one too many, and the brief enumerates the mark. Karl keeps COA Check and its share card, where no rule touches him.
- **D8. The `/compounds/` empty state drops the term it used to quote** ("Nothing here matches 'X'") and the instruction that followed it ("Try the name printed on the vial…"). The count line above still says "0 matches" and the field still holds what was typed; the new line describes, as the brief asks.

**The QR**

- **D9. Error-correction level L, version 4 for every compound.** The canonical URL is 55–74 bytes, which is past level M's version-4 capacity for all but the shortest slugs. L holds every one of them at version 4, so every label's QR is the same size, with the largest modules the corner can carry (0.43 mm, 0.33 mm at a 2 in print). A denser level would mean a smaller module, which matters more on paper than 15% error correction does.
- **D10. The direct URL, not a short redirect route.** `/l/<slug>` would have fitted a version-2 symbol with bigger modules, but it adds a public URL namespace the brief didn't ask for. The QR links to the compound's page itself, as the brief says.
- **D11. No compound, no QR.** The calculator has no picker; the only compound it can know is the page the visit came from. Where there is none the label keeps its old shape, rather than carrying a code to somewhere generic.
- **D12. The label's own white padding is the quiet zone.** Drawing four more modules of white inside a 0.56 in box would cost a fifth of the symbol's size; the label gives the symbol 0.1 in of white on every side, which is six modules.
- **D13. `label-scan` fires on any page opened with `?from=label`,** with `compound: none` where the path is not a compound page. A label's QR can only point at a compound page; firing anyway keeps the event honest if someone edits the address.
- **D14. The test records my own symbols,** having checked them against an independent encoder and a decoder while they were made. Byte-equality with that encoder is not a test the repo can keep (it has no network and no dependencies), and the padding region differs between any two correct encoders anyway.

**The addenda**

- **D15. The homepage card is generated from the author box's string,** as About's opening already is, so three places cannot drift into three different claims.
- **D16. The closing line changed on the compare page too** (158, not 157). The same sentence credited the same team that does not exist.
- **D17. A hero verdict is fixed only where the page's own evidence section contradicts it,** and then it takes the word that matches the card's meter — the level that was derived from the page: No Data, Preclinical, Limited Human Data, Moderate Evidence, FDA Approved. Where a page's verdict is true of another use or another question (FGL, glutathione, noopept, Semax), it stands and is listed. The honest fix for those is a scope line, which this brief did not ask for.
- **D18. The 21 pages renamed by the group keep their Checked dates,** reseeded as the cards and trust sessions did: a group's name and address are a link, not a claim about the compound.
- **D19. The old group id still lands.** `?tag=glp-1-weight-loss-shots` and `#glp-1-weight-loss-shots` map to the new id in the `/compounds/` script, beside the old group anchors from 12a.
- **D20. No card moved group** on the hook check. Two of the four flags were the rename's own doing; the other two qualify an endpoint and a population.

**The sweep**

- **D21. The diagnosis beat the brief's guess, and both fixes shipped.** The dosing table was already scrolling (as `display:block`, which is what leaked its width); the pages were wide because of one unbreakable word. The table now scrolls in a wrapper, which is the right shape regardless, and the words break.
- **D22. Breaking is scoped to the reading surface.** A long word in the hero, a card or the data table has nowhere good to break; inside the prose it does.
- **D23. Inline links in prose are not tap targets.** The two that remain under 44 px are a link inside a sentence and a button that is deliberately invisible.
- **D24. The corrections page's full-page capture is 211,338 px tall,** so the sweep saved its first 4,000 px and its filtered view instead of a 68 MB image.
