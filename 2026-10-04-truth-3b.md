## Look at these first

1. **The Truth 3 report itself had kisspeptin's WADA section wrong.** It said S2.3; WADA's 2026 List
   names kisspeptin under S2.2.1 ("in males"). The page's row and reference were right, its body said
   S2.3. The body now says S2.2.1.
2. **AOD-9604's mechanism contradicted its own source.** The page said it works through β3-adrenergic
   receptors; the mouse study it cites concluded the opposite. The page also described the 12-week
   trial as 71 people on 0.25–1 mg (the published record: 300 people on 1–30 mg), counted 536 in the
   24-week trial (502 were randomized), and called the safety record "unusually clean" without the
   five cancers reported in the 12-week trial (judged unrelated by the investigators). All corrected,
   cited to Stier et al., 2013; the hook says 502.
3. **Cerebrolysin's "200+ trials" is 90 in PubMed** (62 randomized; searched 2026-10-04). Its label
   infuses within 15 minutes (the page said 30–60), names epilepsy as the contraindication (the page
   said status epilepticus), and the Cochrane review's higher rate of non-fatal serious adverse events
   is now stated. The hook and description follow (the title's "44 Countries" is flagged below).
4. **Retatrutide's two Phase 3 papers came out on 2026-09-29** (TRIUMPH-1, NEJM; TRIUMPH-2, Lancet)
   while the page still called them anticipated. Both are added, with their doses and populations.
   Lilly's TRIUMPH-4 release (2025-12-11, not October 2025) prints NCT05869903, which is
   orforglipron's ATTAIN-1; the page keeps the registry's TRIUMPH-4 number. **A wider content update
   is due** (TRIUMPH-3 and other 2026 readouts were announced in releases not read here).
5. **Cagrilintide and CagriSema overstated their diabetes trial.** The Phase 2 trial did not show
   better HbA1c than semaglutide alone (p = 0.075). The pages also misnamed a planned set of trials
   "REDEFINE 3" (it is the cardiovascular outcomes trial), listed SURPASS-CVOT as ongoing (it reported
   in 2025) and eloralintide as Phase 2 (it is in Phase 3). Cagrilintide's half-life is 159–195 hours.
6. **Your two changes are live.** Sermorelin's stance line reads "Once an approved drug (Geref,
   1990–2008); compounded legally today." with its title and description on 1990 (the FAQ, Legal
   Status and FDA Status row already were); GH Stack's sermorelin card follows. BPC-157's hook says
   393 papers, and the description, data table and body give 393 with the 544 records named.
7. **Prerender was broken at HEAD and is fixed.** Vesilute's changelog entry was typed "content" but
   written as a correction, with no homepage text. The homepage's "what changed" line reads the newest
   content entry dated on or before the newest report, so once the Truth 3 report existed, prerender
   stopped on it. The entry now has its line, and the homepage shows it.

## DECISIONS

1. **"GHK-Cu" in the brief is GHK-Cu Fragment.** `ghk-cu.html` was finished in Truth 3 and is not
   on the remaining list; `ghk-cu-fragment.html` is.
2. **Deploy and report after every batch**, not once at the end, so a cut-off leaves the finished
   pages live and listed.
3. **Metas, titles and hooks are fixed when the edited body contradicts them** (Part 4's rule):
   Cerebrolysin (200+ → 90 trials), AOD-9604 (900+ → 893 people; 536 → 502), CagriSema ("no
   head-to-head data yet" → the trial is unpublished), sermorelin (1997 → 1990). Two hooks that
   repeated another card's opening (Vesilute, Vesugen) were reworded so hooks-check passes.
4. **Contradictions outside the Part 1/2 regions are fixed when a primary record settles them**
   (Truth 3's lesson 5), e.g. kisspeptin's half-life row and AOD-9604's sequence row. Unsourced
   claims outside the regions that no record settles are **flagged, not changed** (below).
5. **Cardarine's AICAR card stays.** Its 40% gene-overlap fact appears nowhere else on the page, so
   the echo rule (keep the cited sentence) leaves it.
6. **A corrections entry per page per part where a reader-visible claim changed.** Cardarine,
   larazotide and thymosin alpha-1 only gained citations and one referral line: no entry.
7. **Pair pages follow their profiles.** `scripts/truth3.py sync-pairs` updated 14 notes and removed
   5; one conflict (cagrilintide with semaglutide) was set to the CagriSema trial's own numbers. The
   7 changed reference lists were re-extracted with `stacks/extract.py`'s own function and 10 quick
   facts synced from the profiles; `stacks/generate.py` rewrote the pages (Tier 1 + kept = 225).
8. **Five corrections entries named a slug, not the page** (`ghk-cu-fragment` ×3, `adamax`,
   `vesilute`; three from Truth 3), so /corrections.html showed the slug. Names fixed.
9. **The Vercel CLI was not upgraded** (60.1.3 installed, 62.2.0 published), as in Truth 3. The deploy
   ran from the documented git-less copy.
