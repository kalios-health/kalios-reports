## DECISIONS

**Part 1 — the mark**
1. **Where the artboards came from.** The three artboards were read from your Claude Design canvas: the repo had no copy. They were treated as design data (rule 3).
2. **mark-solid.svg is white on transparent**, like mark.svg.
   - The favicon files put it on the artboard's black tile (rounded, 10% radius), which is how the artboard draws every small size.
   - The tile keeps a white K visible on light tab bars.
3. **mark-solid's dot has radius 40**, the artboard's smallest clean form and the one on the Label artboard (the 40 px form uses 34).
4. **The favicon set is drawn with Pillow from the same geometry**, supersampled, and so are the OG corners. The build never needs a browser.
5. **The OG corner is about the drip's width** (192 px box), not "under 96 px". OG images are always shown scaled down; a feed shows them at a third to half size, so it renders under 96 px in practice.
6. **OG images use a fixed 16-colour palette** (black, ink, red and the steps between them). The adaptive palette turned the small red dot brown; now both dots are exactly `#e63946`.
7. **`/og-default.png` puts mark-solid bottom right**, opposite KALIOS.HEALTH. The spray tag keeps top right.
8. **The painted tag keeps its own red.** It's a painting; "red is #e63946 everywhere" is applied to the three vector files.
9. **The icon links' `?v=` was bumped** (20260502b → 20260928) on the 453 pages that have them. The two compare pages have no icon links and still rely on `/favicon.ico`. Sample of 3 validated first.

**Part 2 — reading surface**

10. **Where the surface starts and ends, per page type.**
    - **Compound and stack pages:** a wrapper around the Gist and the sections.
    - **Compare pages:** the section content only. Their Gist sits inside the page header, so it stays black.
    - **Pair pages:** from "Mechanism overlap" to the compliance note. The status note under the headline stays black.
    - **About:** everything after the lead.
    - **Tracker:** from "Three legal events" to the sources. The results table is the tracker's data table and the related stacks are cards, so both stay black.
    - **Guide:** the article. Its starter cards stay black.
    - **None:** the calculator, stack tool, homepage, `/compounds/`, 404 and the legacy stacks index (an index, not reading).
11. **The paper runs full width**, painted past the text column. The text column is identical in both modes, so switching never re-wraps a line.
12. **Dark mode is pixel-identical to Part 1.** Every token is restated on `.sl-read`. Colours the pages hard-code (Venn diagram, tag chips, evidence tints, tracker events, guide links) go through `--r-*` tokens whose dark values are the pages' own.
    - Checked with a full-page pixel diff of 7 page types at 390 and 1280 px, the new toggle row hidden: identical.
    - The tracker needed one fix: its caution-stripe rule targeted `.tracker-main > h2`.
13. **Paper colours my brief didn't give:**
    - muted `#4f4d48` (7.48:1)
    - strong rules `rgba(10,10,10,.3)`
    - green text `#125427`, for evidence labels and the Venn (8.0:1 on paper, 7.0:1 on its tint)
14. **The toggle.**
    - It sits last in the section nav, right above the paper.
    - It is 44 × 36 px, reads "Aa", and carries `aria-pressed`, with the label "Aa: paper reading surface".
    - Paper is reserved (spec §4), so "on" is shown by a solid ink border, not a paper fill.
    - Without JavaScript the toggle stays hidden and the phone's setting still applies.
15. **The toggle remembers every choice** (Look at these first, 5).
16. **Marking.**
    - `street_lab_migrate.py read-surface` marks the pages; `stacks/generate.py` emits the wrapper.
    - A regenerated pair page is byte-identical to a migrated one, and generator and prerender agree.
    - Sample of 7 validated first. No content dates moved.

**Part 3 — label**

17. **Sheet.** The label prints on a standard sheet at true size, top left, not on a custom 2.6 × 1.2 in page size, so any printer at 100% gives the true size.
18. **Mixed date and use-by.**
    - The mixed date is the print date.
    - The use-by date adds the calculator's own stability note for that compound, taking its first number (the low end of a range): NAD+ 7 days, MOTS-c 14, GHK-Cu 21. Otherwise it adds 28 days, the calculator's general guideline.
    - This keeps the label from promising longer than the calculator says.
19. **The corner mark is mark-solid's shapes in black**, not the white file on its tile, because the label is black on white, as the Label artboard shows.
20. **Fitting.** The draw line is fitted to the label: Anton is measured on a canvas, and again just before printing.
21. **When no label prints.** Stack mode, or no result yet: the page prints as it did before. The calculator's "Checked" date moved to 28 Sep, because the label adds print-only text.

**Part 4 — small fixes**

22. **The 26:** the 25 from the promote report plus 5-amino-1MQ, which is now in `NO_RECONSTITUTION`.
23. **"FDA’s" uses the typographic apostrophe**, as elsewhere on the site. It's stored per compound as `caution_band` in `gen_regulatory_status.py`, and it wins over the status sentence.
24. **Old reports are amended, not rewritten.** The open wording is struck through, not removed.
