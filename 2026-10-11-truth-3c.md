## Look at these first

1. **ARA-290's page said its Phase 2 safety was clean. The trial record says otherwise.** The type 2
   diabetes trial reports four serious adverse events on ARA-290, two judged possibly related: one
   participant's borderline kidney function worsened and the safety committee stopped the drug, and a
   70-year-old man developed severe leg cellulitis two weeks after his last dose and died of a heart
   attack, which the committee judged unrelated. The 64-person trial's posted registry results add
   serious events in 3 of 46 people on the drug against none of 16 on placebo. All of it is now on the
   page, with the investigators' own words.
2. **ARA-290's receptor is disputed, and the page never said so.** An independent 2018 study found no
   binding of ARA-290 to either part of the proposed innate repair receptor, and no association between
   the two receptors with or without it; its authors call the receptor's existence controversial. Added.
3. **Two trials were missing from ARA-290's page and one was listed twice.** The 22-person intravenous
   pilot (Heij 2012), the 12-week diabetic macular edema trial (Lois 2020, in which vision did not
   improve and which was stopped when the drug expired) and the 36-person volunteer study (Cerit 2015)
   are added; "Brines 2020" and "Brines 2014" were one trial, Brines et al., 2015.
4. **Cerebrolysin's "approved in about 44 countries" had no source anywhere.** Not on the page, not on
   its maker's sites (which say "used in over 50 countries", a different claim, and publish no list).
   The page now names the two approvals its sources show: a prescription medicine in Austria, where its
   maker holds the marketing authorisation, and in Russia, from the Russian drug register. The title,
   description, data row, two paragraphs, the Stacks data and one earlier corrections entry followed.
5. **AOD-9604's TGA "cosmetic ingredient notification" is the opposite of what the TGA document says.**
   The only TGA document that names it, released under freedom of information, is a February 2013
   warning letter telling a business selling "AOD 9604 Cream" that such peptides are therapeutic goods
   that may not be supplied without entry in the Australian register. (Australian cosmetic ingredients
   are notified to AICIS, not the TGA.) Cited; the page's claim of a notification is gone.
6. **Argireline is sold at 5–10% in the trials on its page; the safety panel that reviewed it concluded
   "safe … at concentrations up to 0.005%"** and that the data are insufficient above that. The page's
   old line said the panel "has not identified safety concerns". Corrected, quoted and cited.
7. **Two references pointed at papers that do not exist.** Pemvidutide cited a Diabetologia 2018
   MEDI0382 paper (no such paper in PubMed or at that volume and page), and Argireline cited a 2024
   JMIR Dermatology Google Trends analysis that is in neither PubMed nor Crossref. Both cut. A third
   reference pointed at an erratum record instead of the article (Nahra 2021).
8. **Glumitide's Phase 1 paper was credited to the wrong first author** in its reference and seven
   citations (PubMed: Roell, with Pratt last), and its status box missed a Phase 2 trial that has been
   recruiting since November 28, 2025.
9. **Kalios told athletes what to do on ten pages.** Sixteen lines of "Athletes should consult their
   federation" (and one telling pregnant or nursing readers to consult a clinician) are now descriptions
   of what WADA's 2026 List says.

  "Phase II safety was clean", "no stroke, DVT, MI, or PE events" and "injection site reactions — most

## DECISIONS

1. **A page's own sources decide, and where they are silent the claim goes.** Every item on Truth 3b's
   flagged list was either tied to a document read this session or cut; nothing was kept on plausibility.
2. **Where a source could not be read, the page says so rather than guessing**: EGF's two disclosure
   sections (journal login), the 2015 FDA warning letter (no longer published), the BMJ fact check (all
   three routes), bromantane's 1996 detections (no paper read describes them).
3. **The scope widened where the same fault sat on other pages.** Truth 3b flagged Vilon's federation
   line; the same instruction was on ten pages, so all sixteen lines were fixed. Cerebrolysin's "44
   countries" also lived in a corrections entry from October 5, which now names the two countries.
4. **Corrections beyond the flagged list were made when a primary record settled them** (Truth 3's lesson
   5): glumitide's author order and its stale status box, pemvidutide's two bad references, Argireline's
   unfindable Google Trends paper, the CIR concentration limit, GHRP-6's mis-authored 2013 reference.
5. **DSIP's hero stamp moved from "Moderate Evidence" to "Limited Human Data."** Its record is small
   1981–1992 trials, uncontrolled in withdrawal and weak against placebo in insomnia. The evidence
   verdicts are page content, so this is a content change with an entry.
6. **Bromantane's 2009 Russian approval date stays, flagged.** The register (RLS) gives the manufacturer
   and the 2012 text but no approval date, and Russia's state register needs a captcha, so the date could
   not be verified; it was not on the flagged list, so it was left rather than guessed at either way.
7. **The conservative option where a figure had two readings**: zuclomiphene's half-life is "not
   determined" with the two studies' own findings, not "weeks to months"; pemvidutide's half-life row
   says no figure is in the papers cited.
8. **The deploy ran from the documented git-less copy** (`vercel`/`vercel --prod` from `~/kalios` is
   still BLOCKED). `diff -rq` listed only `.git`, `.claude`, `reports` and a stray `art/.DS_Store`.
9. **The Vercel CLI was not upgraded** (60.1.3 installed, 63.1.2 published), as in every session since
   12e.
10. **One pair-data trap avoided again** (Truth 3b decision 11): syncing quick facts copied DS5's
    component-row HTML links into `stacks/data.json`; that value was restored to plain text.
11. **Pair-page dates.** 24 pair pages whose only change was a removed navigation link were reseeded, so
    their "Checked" date did not move; the 28 whose literature tables or quick facts changed did move.
