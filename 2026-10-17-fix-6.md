## DECISIONS

- **D1. No stamp is a status, not a missing stamp.** `IN TRIALS ONLY` is a status of its own (`gen_regulatory_status.IN_TRIALS`, with its basis), mapped to `streetlab.NO_STAMP`. It draws words in ink, never a stamp's border or color.
- **D2. The exception is named, not general.** `NO_STAMP_PAGES` holds RYZ101 with its reason. The card gate and hero_meter admit only what it names, and print it. A second page would need its own entry.
- **D3. The second door for a page with nothing sold** opens the record the page rests on, its Phase 3's registry entry (`NO_STAMP_DOOR`). COA Check's door would have offered to read a lab report for a product no one can buy.
- **D4. The calculator's tag is decided by what the link carries.** A link that equals the device's remembered math is the person's own, so it keeps "Your last math" (`tagFor`). Otherwise every reload of the page, which writes its math into its address, would read "From the link".
- **D5. A correction's date wins where it is later**, in prerender's normal and reseed modes alike. A structural reseed can no longer roll a corrected page's date back.
- **D6. COA's identity rule reads the report's own words first.** A negative result or another compound beats matching masses. The chosen compound is `?c=` where the page was opened from one, otherwise the report's declared compound.
- **D7. Share images read their number from the built page** (stack, pair, tracker, /stacks/), so the image and the page can't disagree. A compound image keeps drawing from the data, and the build stops if the hero draws another number.
- **D8. The receipt's April lists: kept only what the gate can check.** An entry with no identifier, or one that opens another paper, went. Nothing a deployed page shows went: the receipt's own 27 all match.
- **D9. One status-change phrase per cause.** A stance line that changed because the site's reading changed, with no FDA event, says "legal line changed".
