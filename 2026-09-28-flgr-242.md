7. **Reconstitution arithmetic:** 1 mL and 2 mL in the 5 mg vial, at 1.25, 2.5 and 5 mg (the amounts in the stated regimens), labeled "not a recommendation". There's no storage guidance, because no document gives any.

10. **Stack tool and calculator:** FLGR-242 is in neither, and the brief didn't ask. The page says the Stack Builder lists Follistatin-344, not FLGR-242. The primary action goes to the calculator with no preset.
11. **No changelog entry:** the homepage "what changed" line still shows the redesign (Open question 3).
