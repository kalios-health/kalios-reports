## How the pages are built (12f pipeline)

- **Writer.** One agent per page, working from a playbook of G's Truth 3 rules, the larazotide model and a
  template. Shared, rate-limited sources: PubMed, PMC, ClinicalTrials.gov, openFDA, and the WADA 2026 and FDA
  503A/503B lists as text.
- **Verify.** Two independent verifiers: one checks every claim against its source, one checks rules, law and card
  data.
- **Fix and recheck.** A fixer applies or disputes each finding; a fresh verifier rechecks, for up to three rounds.
- **Integrate.** A per-page integrator wires each page into the data files, then prerender, the gates and one
  commit per page.

All tooling is in `_scratch/12f/` (ignored, not deployed).

## DECISIONS

1. **Sample → validate → scale** (CLAUDE.md rule 6). I sampled three pages plus retatrutide, read the first drafts,
   tightened three playbook rules, then scaled to 18. The three rules:
   - a document that merely lists compounds side by side is not a pairing;
   - no predictive "should" in Kalios's voice;
   - an attributed guide figure needs a real page with its URL recorded.
2. **Tags where G's nine groups don't fit:**
   - Bone drugs (teriparatide, abaloparatide) → Tendon & gut repair. Its taxonomy source is Healing & Recovery,
     and it already holds Cartalax. The meter rates healing trials: approved, but not for that use.
   - Approved hormone replacements (EPO, desmopressin) → Hormones & libido, following insulin's precedent.
   - PNC-27, sold for cancer → Immune; no cancer group exists.
3. **One content entry for the batch** at the end, not 21; corrections entries only where an existing page's claim
   changed. A link added to a page is not a correction.
4. **Macimorelin → FDA APPROVED with its own stance line**: "Approved in 2017 as a one-dose test (Macrilen); US
   sales stopped in 2023." The approval is in effect. The gonadorelin route to COMPOUNDING PHARMACY would imply a
   supply that no document shows.
5. **Retatrutide's three stacking cards stay** (tesamorelin, CJC-1295 + ipamorelin, BPC-157). Truth 3 kept them on
   the page's own earlier wording, and the 12f brief scopes the published-pairing rule to new pages. See question 2.
6. **Stopped the workflows at the usage limit** rather than let them run unattended; deployed what was committed.
7. The Vercel CLI was not upgraded (60.1.3 installed, 62.2.0 published), as in earlier sessions.
