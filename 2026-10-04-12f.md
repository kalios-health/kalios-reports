## DECISIONS

1. **Sample → validate → scale** (CLAUDE.md rule 6). I sampled three pages plus retatrutide and tightened three
   playbook rules, then scaled to the rest:
   - a document that lists compounds side by side is not a pairing;
   - no predictive "should" in Kalios's voice;
   - an attributed guide figure needs a real page.
2. **Tags where the nine groups don't fit.**
   - Bone drugs (teriparatide, abaloparatide) → Tendon & gut repair, rated for healing (approved, not for that use).
   - Approved hormone replacements (EPO, desmopressin) → Hormones & libido, insulin's precedent.
   - PNC-27, sold for cancer → Immune.
   - Crystagen, Cortexin and Prostamax join the Bioregulators class (19).
3. **Macimorelin → FDA APPROVED with its own line.** The approval stands: FDA approved a supplement on 2026-07-23.
   But US sales stopped in 2023. Gonadorelin's route to COMPOUNDING PHARMACY would imply a supply no document shows.
4. **Own stance lines.**
   - Retatrutide and cagrilintide: FDA's October 1 statement, under G's keep-the-first-clause rule.
   - Thymosin beta-4: FDA's January 2026 warning letter says it is a biologic, which can't be compounded. It is on
     no list, so "on the do-not-compound list" would be untrue.
5. **Red flags.**
   - **Flagged:** EPO (its boxed warning) and anamorelin (published life-threatening heart rhythms, one with cardiac
     arrest).
   - **Not flagged:** EGF and Cortexin (deaths not attributed to the drug; for G) and BAM15 (the dinitrophenol deaths
     are a class effect, which the rule excludes).
6. **Backlink cards.** A card that states a study result cites it, with the reference added to the host page. A
   pairing that a published human study tested sits under Commonly Stacked With:
   - BPC-157 + thymosin beta-4;
   - GHRP-6 + EGF;
   - Pinealon + Crystagen.

   This came from my uncited Pinealon card, caught by a second verification.
7. **Second-round findings on pages already live were applied as corrections** (decision 1 above, "Look at these
   first"). Integration now waits for a page's last recheck. Crystagen's third fix round belonged to a stopped run;
   everything its last recheck raised was already applied.
8. **Seven new no-reconstitution pages:** DNSP-11, macimorelin, cyclic glycine-proline, B7-33, anamorelin, BAM15 and
   EGF. They have no vial to mix and are oral, cosmetic or never given to a person. That makes 39.
9. **One content entry for the batch, corrections entries only where a claim changed.** A link added to a page is
   not a correction.
10. **Matrixyl's hook corrected.** It is not one of G's verbatim hooks, and "independent" contradicted its own page.
11. **Retatrutide's three stacking cards stay.** The brief scopes the published-pairing rule to new pages.
12. **llms.txt: counts only**, as for larazotide and Vesilute; the file features ten compounds, not every page.
13. **Reports.** This report keeps the brief's name. `2026-10-05-12f-questions.md` carries the open questions, and
    its date moves the homepage's "what changed" line to the batch.
14. **Code changes, small:**
    - `scripts/goals.py`'s class sentence handles a class with controlled trials and no approval (Cortexin);
    - `scripts/streetlab.py` ranks a phase-less registered trial below phased ones in the Pipeline row.
15. The Vercel CLI was not upgraded (60.1.3; 62.2.0 published), as in earlier sessions.
16. **A separate verifier read my own text before the deploy:** every corrections entry dated October 5 (checked
    against the old and new pages in git) and every card I wrote by hand. Its 31 findings were applied after I
    checked each one against the source. Three changes go beyond the log:
