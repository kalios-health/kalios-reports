- **D4. "No human hormone by that name."** A PubMed search for GLP-3 found "The chondrichthyan glucagon-like peptide 3 regulates hepatic ketone metabolism in the Pacific spiny dogfish" (Gen Comp Endocrinol 2024; PMID 38346454), and a 2018 review of fish proglucagon genes (PMID 29510149). Your sentence holds for people, so it says so. The cited paper is unchanged.
- **D5. No corrections entry for (c).** Nothing on the page was wrong, and the brief asks for entries only in (d). The page's date moved (its text changed).
