## DECISIONS

All taken on the conservative side, no questions asked.

**Groups**
- **D1. The primary-group rule.** A class member's primary group is its class; every other compound's is its use tag.
  - "Bioregulators stays a group" only works if its members' cards sit there. Otherwise it would be a page of links.
  - So all 19 Khavinson peptides sit in Bioregulators, Epithalon and Cortexin included. Their use groups list them under Also relevant.
- **D2. Who goes in GLP-1 & Weight-Loss Shots.** G's definition: "the incretin and amylin drugs and their analogues".
  - Orforglipron is in: a pill, but a GLP-1 receptor agonist. So is glumitide, a GIP-only agonist.
  - Setmelanotide (an MC4R agonist) and bimagrumab (an antibody) are neither, so they are Fat-Loss Peptides.
  - MOTS-c's and tesamorelin's secondary "Fat loss" became Fat-Loss Peptides.
- **D3. Where "Also relevant" goes.** It is the goal page's last line, as the brief says ("ends with"): after the alert form, ordered like the cards. It is not on `/compounds/`, which shows each compound once and nothing else.
- **D4. The goal sentence counts the cards on the page**, so it matches what the page shows. Longevity now reads "Of 8", not "Of 16": its Khavinson peptides are under Bioregulators.
  - The fat-loss halves say what they hold, so neither claims to be every fat-loss compound.
  - The two halves link each other under their sentence. That wasn't asked; it keeps one click between what used to be one page.
- **D5. `/goals/fat-loss.html` 301s to Fat-Loss Peptides,** the group that keeps the name.
  - On `/compounds/`, `#fat-loss` lands on GLP-1 & Weight-Loss Shots, the first of the two halves, which sit side by side.
  - `?tag=fat-loss` shows both halves; the old links from compound pages use it.
  - The retired page's one corrections entry links the redirect target.
- **D6. Title Case where pages are generated; page prose untouched.** The brief says page prose stays as Truth 3c left it. Two prose mentions keep sentence case: clomiphene's "the Hormones & libido group" and teriparatide's "Tendon & gut repair". So does the hand-written "See every … compound →" link in 154 compound pages' Next-steps sections (open question 3).
  - The primary action keeps G's sentence with the group in Title Case: "See every Fat-Loss Peptides compound".

**The card**
- **D7. The route word rule.** The word says how the compound is taken for the use on its card:
  - the form it is sold in, or, for a trial drug, the form its trials gave;
  - the schedule from its page's dosing section.
  - **DAILY SHOT** = injected every day or every other day. hCG's 500 IU every other day is a DAILY SHOT.
  - **WEEKLY SHOT** = injected once to three times a week, or every two weeks. TB-500's twice-weekly loading and weekly maintenance is a WEEKLY SHOT, and so is thymosin alpha-1 twice a week.
  - **PILL** = anything by mouth, including macimorelin's granules for a drink. **CREAM** = anything on the skin. **NASAL SPRAY** = sprays and nasal drops.
  - Where no word in the set is true, the card shows none rather than a wrong one (Part 2 lists the 20).
- **D8. The "Checked" date.** The dot is gone everywhere. G's 30-day window is kept: the brief changes the dot, not the window (open question 7).
- **D9. The amber.** `#f28c28` replaced the gold in `--crx`. That token was named for COMPOUNDED RX and, until now, colored the mid-level verdict stamps, which are white now.
  - The markup writes "Compounded Rx"; CSS sets it in capitals.
  - Changelog and corrections entries that name COMPOUNDING PHARMACY are history and keep it.
- **D10. Which verdict stamps turned white.** All 120 that aren't "FDA Approved". Green means FDA APPROVED and nothing else, so the 27 green "FDA Approved" verdicts, all on FDA APPROVED compounds, stay green.
- **D11. "for another use"** goes on every FDA APPROVED compound outside `APPROVED_FOR`, the existing table of approvals that cover the card's use: 9. It goes on the card, under the stamp.
- **D12. Which heroes get a scope line.** Only where a page's own text puts human trials, or an approval, on a use other than the card's: 16. The wording follows G's example in both directions.
  - Left out:
    - N-Acetyl Epithalon, N-Acetyl Selank and N-Acetyl Semax, whose verdicts describe their parent molecules, not another use (open question 6);
    - TB-500, whose human data are on its parent;
    - Semax, whose Russian approval covers cognitive uses as well as stroke;
    - glutathione, whose "Clinical Use" names no single use.
- **D13. One legal line.** On every page that has the line, it now carries the hero's stance line, word for word.
  - Where the line rests on an FDA list, it links that list. 10 pages keep a source link there.
  - GHRP-2, GHRP-6 and MK-677 had links to two lists and now link the one the line rests on; their Legal Status sections still name both lists.

**Order and data**
- **D14. Tier dividers.** Each divider shows the stamp's words and color, with a count. An empty tier is left out rather than shown as "None.". Goal pages lose their evidence-level headings so that the order is the same everywhere, from one function.
- **D15. `data/demand.json`.** The seed is scored by rank, 44 down to 1, and every unlisted compound or stack is 0. It is a build input, not deployed (`.vercelignore`), as `routes.json` is.
- **D16. FLGR-242** goes into `stacks/data.json` as `lookup_only`, like every page added since 12b, so it gets no pair page.
- **D17. `/stacks-index.html`** is retired with kind `legacy`. Its page kind is removed from `scripts/streetlab.py`.

**Dates, records and checks**
- **D18. Checked dates.** The 21 pair pages were redrawn twice (Title Case tags, then the new card) and keep their dates, as in the prune.
  - The only page whose Checked date moved for content is the beginner's guide, whose card description I rewrote.
  - Hub and goal pages move with their cards, as always; all were already dated 2026-10-11.
- **D19. No corrections entries.** Nothing that changed was false: retatrutide's old line ("not on any FDA 503A list") was true, if incomplete, and "Preclinical" beside thymosin beta-4's eye trials was not wrong. One site changelog entry describes the session.
- **D20. One agent.** My standing note asks for an independent agent to check hand-written card text before a deploy. The brief says one agent, no parallel work, so I did that check myself.
  - What I checked: the 16 scope lines, the route-word reasons and the GLP-1 list, each read against its page, and the key words of each scope line found on its page by script.
- **D21. IndexNow.** The script picks retired URLs by date, so `--retired 2026-10-11` sent the prune's 168 again with today's 2. A repeat submission is harmless.
- **D22. The report's date.** It is named 2026-10-12, as the brief asks. Pages, data and deploy say 2026-10-11, the day the work ran.
